Preinstalled apps grouped by what happens if you switch them off. APEX reads this from its own list and never touches anything marked as required by the system.

Snapshots

A snapshot records which apps are on or off right now. Restoring one puts every app back exactly as it was, so you can try things and walk them back.

Backups are written to Download/APEX as a single archive holding the app files and, if you ask for it, its data.
